# Source Notes

## NHTSA archives

The official archive URLs are stored in the source log and acquisition script. The large raw files are not bundled because the execution runtime could not reliably transfer them. A verified excerpt is included only to execute schema and quality logic.

NHTSA warns that reports differ by telemetry, vehicle type, geography, exposure, and reporting requirement. Initial reports may be incomplete or revised.

## California DMV

The project uses program totals for the 2023 and 2024 reporting periods. DMV states that disengagement reports are not designed for company comparisons and omit simulation, private roads, out of state testing, and testing below SAE Level 3.

## Waymo

The 56.7 million rider only mile study is used because it includes an exposure denominator and matched human benchmarks. It remains company authored and limited to Waymo operating domains.

## Tesla

The 2024 Form 10 K is the primary architecture and scale source. Deliveries are not used as an autonomous mile denominator. Mutable safety and AI pages are treated as supporting context only.

## NVIDIA

The Form 10 K and dated DRIVE releases support platform and commercial claims. Partner adoption does not create a homogeneous fleet safety metric.

## Mobileye

The Form 10 K supports installed base, shipment, product tier, and business model claims. Systems shipped span multiple automation levels.

## Cloud pricing

Exact historical cloud pricing is difficult to archive consistently. Official provider pages and APIs establish configuration and pricing mechanisms. Dated secondary corroboration is labeled as such. All prices are retained as scenario snapshots, not invoices.
