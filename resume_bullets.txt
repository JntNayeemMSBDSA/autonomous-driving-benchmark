LinkedIn project description
Reconstructed and substantially expanded an earlier Microsoft client-based global internship project as an independent, publicly reproducible benchmark of Tesla, Waymo, NVIDIA, and Mobileye. I classified the companies by ecosystem role before comparing their sensor strategies, data workflows, in-vehicle compute, training and simulation infrastructure, deployment evidence, safety indicators, commercial models, and scalability constraints. The project includes an executed SQLite database, reproducible Python scripts and notebooks, SQL analytical views, standardized cloud GPU cost scenarios, sensitivity analysis, Power BI-ready data, dashboard mockups, a source register, and automated quality controls. It does not present the four companies as identical competitors, estimate confidential company spending, or claim that the reconstructed outputs are the exact historical deliverables submitted to Microsoft.

Portfolio description
A role-aware analytics benchmark of autonomous-driving operators, consumer ADAS developers, compute-platform providers, and OEM technology suppliers. The project integrates dated public regulatory, technical, financial, and company evidence through June 30, 2025; separates raw, staging, cleaned, reporting, and quality layers; and demonstrates Python, SQL, relational modeling, scenario analysis, sensitivity testing, data visualization, source governance, and Power BI design. The main finding is that scalability follows different paths across consumer distribution, geofenced Level 4 operations, horizontal compute and simulation platforms, and OEM technology integration, so no single safety or technology ranking is defensible from the available public data.
